A Historic Milestone for Global Ministry

On November 10, 2017, a momentous occasion unfolded in Houston, Texas, as Jim Gillett, International Director, and Dr. Sunny Mathew performed the ribbon-cutting ceremony at the new International Headquarters Building of Ireland Outreach International.

This landmark event marked a significant expansion in the ministry's global reach while honoring its founding roots and mission. The establishment of the international headquarters in the United States represents a new chapter in Ireland Outreach's commitment to serve communities worldwide.

Continuing the Mission

While this Houston facility serves as the international hub, Ireland Outreach International maintains its vital presence in Ireland, staying true to its origins and the community that shaped its vision.

Our Global Mission

Ireland Outreach International continues to:

  • Demonstrate the love of God through compassionate action and service
  • Preach the Word of God with clarity and conviction
  • Expand our reach across nations and cultures
  • Maintain our roots in Ireland while serving globally

A Testament to Faith and Vision

The opening of the International Headquarters Building stands as a testament to years of faithful ministry and God's provision. This facility enables Ireland Outreach to coordinate its global efforts more effectively, supporting missions and outreach programs around the world.

From its humble beginnings to this significant milestone, Ireland Outreach International has remained steadfast in its dual commitment: demonstrating God's love through action and proclaiming His Word with power.

Looking Forward

As we celebrate this achievement, we remain focused on the work ahead. With headquarters on two continents, Ireland Outreach International is better positioned than ever to fulfill its calling to reach the nations with the Gospel of Jesus Christ.
