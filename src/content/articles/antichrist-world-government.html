DANIEL

Daniel was carried off to Babylon as a royal hostage in 605 B. C. He rose rapidly to become a leading statesman in Nebuchadnezzar's government.

Daniel's prophecies make more sense when we understand the structure of his book. Daniel wrote his first chapter in Hebrew, and the chapter serves as an introduction to the book. In it we learn how Daniel came to be in Babylon, and why he received God's blessing on his life. But beginning in chapter two, Daniel changes both languages and topics. In Daniel 2:4, Daniel switches from writing in Hebrew to writing in Aramaic ("Then the astrologers answered the king in Aramaic"). The languages are similar, but the effect on Daniel's readers is as dramatic as if he had switched from writing in English to writing in Greek. Daniel wrote chapters two to seven in Aramaic before switching back to Hebrew at the beginning of chapter eight.

Why the switch in language? In chapters two to seven Daniel wrote of events important to all nations, so he wrote in the common commercial language of his day. Beginning in chapter eight, he focused on events related especially to the people of Israel, so he switched back to Hebrew. Chapters two to seven focus on events relating to the "times of the Gentiles".

God allowed Nebuchadnezzar and the Babylonians to destroy the Kingdom of Judah and carry off the Jews into captivity. God's purpose for the nation of Israel had always been the establishment of His kingdom on earth with the coming of Israel's Messiah. But because of the nation's disobedience, God punished the people by allowing them to be defeated and deported. Daniel wrote to announce to the Jews that in spite of their circumstances, God was still in control and working out His plans for the world. God's kingdom would yet come, and the Jews would one day be restored to the land of Israel.

Daniel wrote the Aramaic section of his book to explain to the Jews that God's kingdom was still coming. Daniel interpreted Nebuchadnezzar's dream in (Daniel 2:24):

Therefore Daniel went in unto Arioch, whom the king had ordained to destroy the wise men of Babylon: he went and said thus unto him; Destroy not the wise men of Babylon: bring me in before the king, and I will show unto the king the interpretation.

He also explained the meaning of the stone that destroyed Nebuchadnezzar's statue. In chapter seven, Daniel pictured "one like a son of man" coming to destroy the beasts roaming the world (Daniel 7:14):

And there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve Him: His dominion is an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away, and His kingdom that which shall not be destroyed.

In Daniel chapters two to seven, the prophet explains what will happen between the time when the kingdom of Judah was destroyed, and God's future kingdom is finally established. This is the period commonly called the "times of the Gentiles" - a time when Gentile powers rule over the Jews and control the land of Israel and the city of Jerusalem.

THE FOUR NATIONS

In the book of Daniel chapters two to seven, the prophet provides insight into the Gentile powers that will rule Israel and the Jews from the time of Nebuchadnezzar until the coming of God's kingdom for Israel. Chapter two pictures the nations as four parts of a giant statue, while chapter seven describes the nations as four beasts climbing from the sea.

After describing the statue smashed by a stone, Daniel interprets the vision (Daniel 2:36): This is the dream; and we will tell the interpretation thereof before the king.

In chapter seven, the vision confuses Daniel until an angel comes and interprets it for him (Daniel 7:16): I came near unto one of them that stood by, and asked him the truth of all this. So he told me, and made me know the interpretation of the things.

What was the four-part statue seen by Nebuchadnezzar in his dream in Daniel two? Daniel explains each part to the king. The head of gold represented Nebuchadnezzar himself (Daniel 2:38):

And wheresoever the children of men dwell, the beasts of the field and the fowls of the heaven hath He given into thine hand, and hath made thee ruler over them all. Thou art this head of gold.

The times of the Gentiles began when Nebuchadnezzar invaded, conquered, and deported the Jews from Israel. This took place in 587 B. C. Nebuchadnezzar, as the leader of the country that destroyed Israel, took top honors in the line of Gentile nations.

Having interpreted the head of gold, Daniel moved to the second part of the vision- the chest and arms of silver. Nebuchadnezzar's Babylonian Empire would be replaced by another kingdom. On October 12, 539 B. C., Babylon fell to Cyrus, king of the Medo-Persian Empire (Daniel 5:30-31): In that night was Belshazzar the king of the Chaldeans slain. And Darius the Median took the kingdom, being about threescore and two years old.

Daniel described this second empire as "the kings of Media and Persia" (Daniel 8:20): The ram which thou sawest having two horns are the kings of Media and Persia.

After Daniel interpreted the meaning of the first two sections of the statue in chapter two, he described the third part of the vision. The statue had "its belly and thighs of bronze" (Daniel 2:32): This image's head was of fine gold, his breast and his arms of silver, his belly and his thighs of brass. . .

Daniel's explanation of the belly of bronze is very brief (Daniel 2:39): And after thee shall arise another kingdom inferior to thee, and another third kingdom of brass, which shall bear rule over all the earth.

Daniel confirms the sequence in chapter eight when he names the empire that would follow Medo-Persia. Daniel identifies the empire pictured in chapter eight as a "goat" who would destroy the Medo-Persian Empire (Daniel 8:21): And the rough goat is the king of Grecia: and the great horn that is between his eye is the first king.

Greece replaced Medo-Persia in 330 B. C. Daniel describes the first three empires in the "times of the Gentiles" as being Babylon, Medo-Persia, and Greece. All three controlled the Middle East and ruled over the land of Israel. Daniel finally comes to the fourth part of his vision, the "legs of iron". Daniel again explains that the new metal on the statue represents a kingdom (Daniel 2:40): And the fourth kingdom shall be strong as iron: forasmuch as iron breaketh in pieces and subdueth all things: and as iron that breaketh all these, shall it break in pieces and bruise.

Daniel doesn't name this fourth kingdom in his book, but the empire that defeated Greece, conquered the Middle East, and controlled the land of Israel was Rome. Daniel explained that when the kingdom of God –pictured as a stone –finally came, "it struck the statue on its feet of iron and clay and smashed them" (Daniel 2:34): Thou sawest till that a stone was cut out without hands, which smote the image upon his feet that were of iron and clay, and brake them to pieces.

This fourth empire would be the empire ruling over Israel when God's kingdom comes to earth. When Jesus came to Israel and announced in Matthew 4:17, "Repent: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand", Rome was ruling over Israel.

In Daniel chapter seven, the prophet describes the four nations as beasts emerging from the sea. The description may be different, but the same nations are in view.

Jesus Christ will rule the earth with righteousness and peace, in which the Jews will be leaders, and all the nations will receive great spiritual and economic blessings. The main concept of Old Testament prophecy is the kingdom of God on earth. For this reason, Israel was chosen as God's people. Jesus Christ was born in Israel as the true Messiah of the Jews.

The Scripture discloses that the dead in christ shall be raised from the dust of the ground. The living saints will be transformed. From the heart of the earth and from the depths of the sea, we shall live in bodies resurrected , glorified, immortalized, and. Another disclosure is that the Lord shall personally appear out of heaven, and shall set up in this new and glorified earth a kingdom that shall stand forever.

The doctrine of the coming Millennial kingdom of Christ is one of the greatest revelations in the Bible. The Millennium is the reign of Christ for one thousand years on the earth, following His Second Coming. The reign of Christ or the kingdom of Christ never ends-even after one thousand years. The Bible teaches that Christ's kingdom will be an eternal one, which will never end. The Millennium is only chapter one of the eternal kingdom of Christ. Revelation chapter twenty gives us a vivid account of His Millennial kingdom.

In the book of Revelation chapter 20:1-3, it states:

And I saw an angel come down from heaven, having the key of the bottomless pit and a great chain in his hand. And he laid hold on the dragon, that old serpent, which is the Devil, and Satan, and bound him a thousand years, And cast him into the bottomless pit, and shut him up, and set a seal upon him, that no more, till the thousand years should be fulfilled: and after that he must be loosed a little season.

Jesus Christ will be the future ruler of the whole world. God created Adam to rule the world, but due to sin, Adam failed to fulfill His original purpose. God's purpose, however, is fulfilled in Jesus Christ. God declares His purpose to set His king in Zion as is stated in Psalm 2:6-9: Yet have I set My king upon My holy hill of Zion. I will declare the decree: the Lord hath said unto Me, Thou art My son; this day have I begotten Thee. Ask of Me, and I shall give Thee the heathen for thine inheritance, and the uttermost parts of the earth for Thy possession. Thou shalt break them with a rod of iron; thou shalt dash them in pieces like a potter's vessel.

In Daniel 2:35, a stone which fills the whole earth is an anticipation of the universal rule of Christ: Then was the iron, the clay, the brass, the silver, and the gold, broken to pieces together, and became like the chaff of the summer threshingfloors; and the wind carried them away, that no place was found for them: and the stone that smote the image became a great mountain, and filled the whole earth.

Furthermore, Daniel 7:14 makes Christ's kingdom clear: And there was given Him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve Him: His dominion is an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away, and His kingdom that which shall not be destroyed.

In the Millennial kingdom, the righteousness and justice of God will finally be experienced in this world. Man will be tested during this period as he lives under the government of God, without the active temptation of Satan, who was bound in the bottomless pit. But the descendants of the decendents of the Jews and gentiles saved during the tribulation. who entered the Millennium will still be born under the curse, and will not be immune from sin. Any sin will be dealt with separately by Christ as He rules with "a rod of iron". Even in his ideal condition, man will fail his final test.

At the end of the Millennium, Jesus Christ will surrender the kingdom to His father as I Corinthians 15:24 states: Then cometh the end, when He shall have delivered up the kingdom to God, even the Father; when He shall have put down all rule and all authority and power.

WHO WILL ENTER THE MILLENNIUM

The people who enter the millennial rule of Christ at its inception, will consist of those who survive the severe judgment of both Israel and the Gentiles. All the wicked will be put to death after the Second Coming of Christ, and only saints who have lived through the preceding time of trouble will be eligible for entrance into the Millennial kingdom.

This judgment of the Gentiles, where only the righteous are permitted to enter the Millennial kingdom, is described in Matthew 25:31-46: When the Son of man shall come in His glory, and all the holy angels with Him, then shall He sit upon the throne of His glory: And before Him shall be gathered all nations: and He shall separate them one from another, as a shepherd divideth his sheep from the goats: And He shall set the sheep on His right hand, but the goats on the left. Then shall the King say unto them on His right hand, Come, ye blessed of My Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world: For I was an hungered, and ye gave me meat; I was thirsty, and ye gave Me drink; I was a stranger, and ye took Me in: Naked, and ye clothed me: I was sick, and ye visited me: I was in prison, and ye came unto me. Then shall the righteous answer Him, saying, Lord, when saw we Thee an hungered, and fed Thee? or thirsty, and gave Thee drink? When saw we Thee a stranger, and took Thee in? or naked, and clothed Thee? Or when saw we Thee sick, or in prison, and came unto Thee? And the King shall answer and say unto them, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto Me. Then shall He say also unto them on the left hand, Depart from Me, ye cursed, into everlasting fire, prepared for the devil and his angels: For I was an hungered, and ye gave Me no meat; I was thirsty, and ye gave Me no drink; I was a stranger. and ye took Me not in: naked, and ye clothed Me not: sick, and in prison, and ye visited Me not. Then shall they also answer Him, saying, Lord, when saw we Thee an hungered, or athirst, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and did not minister unto Thee? Then shall He answer them, saying, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye did it not to Me. And these shall go away into everlasting punishment: but the righteous into life eternal.

According to Ezekiel 20:33-38, God will also deal with Israel and purge out all rebels, that is unbelievers, permitting only the saints among Israel to enter the Millennial kingdom: As I live, saith the Lord God, surely with a mighty hand, and with a stretched out arm, and with fury poured out, will I rule over you: And I will bring you out from the people, and will gather you out of the countries wherein ye are scattered, with a mighty hand, and with a stretched out arm, and with fury poured out. And I will bring you into the wilderness of the people, and there will I plead with you face to face. Like as I pleaded with your fathers in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so will I plead with you, saith the Lord God. And I will cause you to pass under the rod, and I will bring you into the bond of the covenant: And I will purge out from among you the rebels, and them that transgress against Me: I will bring them forth out of the country where they sojourn, and they shall not enter into the land of Israel: and ye shall know that I am the Lord.

The parable of the wheat and the tares in Matthew 13:30 states: Let both grow together until the harvest: and in the time of harvest I will say to the reapers, Gather ye together first the tares, and bind them in bundles to burn them: but gather the wheat into my barn.

The parable of the good and bad fish in Matthew 13: 49-50 states: So shall it be at the end of the world: the angels shall come forth, and sever the wicked from among the just, And shall cast them into the furnace of fire: there shall be wailing and gnashing of teeth.

Both of these parables teach likewise that only the wheat and the good fish, representing the righteous, will survive the judgment. We can also see supporting Scriptures in Isaiah 65:11-66:16 and Jeremiah 25:30-33.

According to Isaiah 63:1-6, the Gentiles will be put down in that day, and many of them will be put to death: Who is this that cometh from Edom, with dyed garments from Bozrah? this that is glorious in His apparel, travelling in the greatness of His strength? I that speak in righteousness, mighty to save. Wherefore art Thou red in Thine apparel, and Thy garments like Him that treadeth in the winevat? I have trodden the winepress alone; and of the people there was none with Me: for I will tread them in Mine anger, and trample them in My fury; and their blood shall be sprinkled upon My garments, and I will stain all My raiment. For the day of vengeance is in My heart, and the year of My redeemed is come. And I looked, and there was none to help; and I wondered that there was none to uphold: therefore My own arm brought salvation unto Me; and My fury, it upheld Me. And I will tread down the people in Mine anger, and make them drunk in My fury, and I will bring down their strength to the earth.

Joel 3:1, 2, and 12 also state: For, behold, in those days, and in that time, when I shall bring again the captivity of Judah and Jerusalem, I will also gather all nations, and will bring them down into the valley of Jehoshaphat, and will plead with them there for My people and for My heritage Israel, whom they have scattered among the nations, and parted My land. Let the heathen be wakened, and come up to the valley of Jehoshaphat: for there will I sit to judge all the heathen round about.

This judgment is further expressed in Zephaniah 3:8 and Zechariah 14:1-19. The time specified for the judgment of the Gentiles is simply stated as being "When the Son of man shall come in His glory " (Matthew 25:31). The place of the judgment is in the earth. The entire world will be affiliated with the Antichrist-the Beast of Revelation thirteen. The basis of the judgment of the Gentiles is declared in the passage previously quoted in Matthew 25:40-46. The sheep assisted the Jews, and the goats did not assist them. They will not be saved just for their works of assisting Jews. They assist the Jews because they will put their trust in Christ, and the assistance is the fruit of their salvation. This is the reason that the believing Gentiles will also enter into the Millennium.

THE JUDGMENT OF LIVING ISRAEL

The remnant of Israel, those who survive the Tribulation and are on the earth at the time of the return of Christ, are the specific objects of judgment as Zechariah 13:8-9 states: And it shall come to pass, that in all the land, saith the Lord, two parts therein shall be cut off and die; but the third shall be left therein. And I will bring the third part through the fire, and will refine them as silver is refined, and will try them as gold is tried: they shall call on My name, and I will hear them: I will say, It is My people: and they shall say, The Lord is my God.

Note also Ezekiel 20: 33-38. In the prophecies of Malachi, a refining of the sons of Levi is predicted at the time of Christ's coming (Malachi 3: 2-5). This conclusion is confirmed by the statements and parables of Matthew chapters twenty-four and twenty-five.

The result of the Ezekiel judgment is that the rebels are put to death, and they will be raised from the dead at the judgment of the Great White Throne after the Millennium, to participate in the destiny of all the wicked from Cain onward. The blessings of the entrance to the promised land is given in Jeremiah 31:10-13.

MILLENNIAL GOVERNMENT

Isaiah chapter eleven gives a graphic picture of the reign of Christ on the earth. For supporting verses, see Isaiah 42:4, Jeremiah 23:3-6, Daniel 2:35-45, and Zechariah 14:1-9.

The covenant which God made with Abraham, and the kingdom promises to David, Solomon, and all the prophets will be finally realized in the Millennial kingdom. The blessings for Israel and the Gentiles will be fulfilled when Christ becomes the ruler in Jerusalem. As the true Son of David, He will take His rightful place upon the throne of David. (The resurrected David will be the sub-ruler to Christ in Israel). Christ will receive the worship of men, and fulfill each of the remaining prophecies of the Old Testament.

One of the most wonderful characteristics of life during this period is that God's Holy Spirit will be poured out on Israel and the Gentile nations to transform mankind. (Joel 2:28-29). The infilling of the Holy Spirit gave birth to the church on the Day of Pentecost, two thousand years ago. It was only the foretaste of the Millennial kingdom, in which the Spirit will renew the spirits of all natural men who are believers.

As the Lord pours out His Spirit upon Israel, her heart will overflow with joy. The Gentile nations will bask in the everflowing blessings of Israel, and will share in the glory of the Millennium. All wars will cease, and ultimate peace will be established (Micah 4:3).

Under the direction of Jesus Christ, the resurrected believers of the Church will provide the leadership necessary to create a just society. The curse which was placed upon the earth following the sin of Adam and Eve, will be lifted when the kingdom is established (Isaiah 33:24). All sickness will be eliminated (Isaiah 29:17, 18). The prophet Isaiah writes, "The earth shall be full of the knowledge of Jehovah as the waters cover the sea (Isaiah 11:9). God will forgive their sins (Jeremiah 31:33-34). Weapons will also be eliminated (Isaiah 2:4).

SOCIAL, POLITICAL, AND ECONOMIC ASPECTS OF THE MILLENNIUM

The cessation of war will have a beneficial effect upon both the social and economic life of the world. Military build-up will become unnecessary, and the money will be utilized for the welfare of the people.

Salvation is very common, as the majority of the earth's population will be saved. In the beginning of the Millennium, the enemies of Christ will be destroyed (Revelation 19:11-21). The Millennial period begins with a society in which both Jews and Gentiles are saved. As children are born into their homes, however, even though they are in the favorable circumstances of the Millennial reign of Christ, many of them will not be born again. These will rebel against Christ at the end of the Millennium (Revelation 20:8, 9).

In the Millennium, widespread peace and prosperity will prevail (Jeremiah 31:12, Ezekiel 34:25-27, Joel 2: 21, 27, Amos 9: 13, 14). Those who have illness and are disabled will be healed (Isaiah 33:24 and 61: 1-3). The book of Isaiah speaks of the death of a person one hundred years old, as the death of a child (Isaiah 65:20). The fertility rate will increase dramatically.

GEOGRAPHICAL CHANGE

Many topographical changes will take place in the land of Palestine in connection with the establishment of the Millennial reign of Christ (Zechariah 14:3, 4). Changes in the earth will elevate Jerusalem above the surrounding territory (Zechariah 14: 5, 10). All the land will be depressed in order that Jerusalem might be elevated (Isaiah 2:2 and Micah 4:1).

THE GREAT WHITE THRONE JUDGMENT

For many people, the saddest and most hopeless eternity can be found in Revelation 20: 11-15. At the end of the Millennium, a Great White Throne is established. It may be in space, as both the earth and the starry heavens flee away from it in the destruction of the present earth and heaven. All the wicked who are dead, from Cain to the last, small and great will be raised from the grave. They are judged according to their works, as written in "the books," which is the divine record of human activity. Even a good person, like Mahatma Gandhi, if he did not accept Jesus Christ as his personal Savior, would be included in this Great White Throne judgment. They will be cast into the lake of fire (Revelation 20:15). This is defined as the second death (Revelation 20:16). Physical death is a separation of the soul from the body, so the second death is eternal separation of the wicked from God.

All the angels who joined in Satan's rebellion, will stand before the Great White Throne. These evil angels belong to two classes. One group of fallen angels sinned in a particular manner before the flood and attempted physical relations with women. They were chained by God in a special dark prison (II Peter 2:4 and Jude verse 6). The second group of rebellious angels were allowed to follow Satan (Matthew 25:41). They will be cast into the lake of fire.

THE NEW HEAVEN AND THE NEW EARTH

According to Revelation 21:10, following the judgment of the Great White Throne, a new heaven and a new earth are created to replace the present earth and heaven. It is mentioned in Isaiah 65:17 and Revelation 21:1. There is no more sea, and all sorrows and sufferings will end.

The new earth is the dispensation of the "fulness of times" which is described in Ephesians 1:10: That in the dispensation of the fulness of times He might gather together in one all things in Christ, both which are in heaven, and which are on earth; even in Him.

After the Millennium, the existing earth and heavens will be cleansed with fire, and renewed from the corruption of sin (II Peter 3: 7, 10-13). Then Satan will be released for a little while to test mankind one last time. Many of those born during this period of the natural men and women, will choose to join this final rebellion of Satan. God will annihilate Satan and his army with fire. Death and destruction will be no more.

The New Jerusalem, the home of the believers, will descend to the new earth for all eternity. It will be the home of the believers in this dispensation, as we reign with Christ forever. The curse of sin will be eradicated forever. Creation will be transformed into a Worldwide Garden of Eden. All animals as well as people will become vegetarians. (This condition will also exist during the Millennium.) Life on the new earth will be more enjoyable, and be beyond our imagination and dreams. Christians will be like Jesus Christ (I John 3:2).

The new heavens and the New Jerusalem will be the ultimate home of all saved believers from Adam to the last saved one on earth. The tree of life that was removed after Adam's sin, will be brought back. The New Jerusalem will be illuminated by the Divine Light of God. The greatest joy of the believers will be that we will see His face. We will live with Jesus forever.

THE SUN AND THE MOON

The prevailing view among most Christians is that there shall be no sun or moon to lighten the new earth because they will have been destroyed. Revelation 21:23 states: And the city (New Jerusalem) had no need of the sun or of the moon to shine in it, for the glory of God illuminated it, and the Lamb is its light.

This does not mean that there will be no more sun or moon at all, but that it is impossible for their light to penetrate the overwhelming brightness of God's glory within the city. Isaiah 60:19 reads: The sun shall no longer be your light by day, nor for brightness shall the moon give light to you; but the Lord will be to you an everlasting light.

The eternity of the sun and moon in the aspects of day and night are also seen in reference to the eternal punishment of Gehenna hell. Revelation 20:10 says: And the devil who deceived them, was cast into the lake of fire and brimstone where the beast (Antichrist) and the false prophet are. And they will be tormented day and night forever and ever. Also, in Revelation 14:10-11, it says that those who worship the beast will be tormented day and night. Day and night have no relevance to eternity unless they are literal. Thus the light of the sun and moon will exist to mark hell's continuing existence. Their light will be upon the new earth to assure the earth's covenant of perpetual production of vegetation. Genesis 8:22 states: While the earth remains, seedtime and harvest, and cold and heat, and winter and summer, and day and night shall not cease.

THE AGES OF THE AGES

The following section is quoted from Clarence Larkin: " After the entrance of the new earth, time will cease to exist, and the eternal ages called the "ages of the ages" will begin. The ages to come is described in Ephesians 2 :7: That in the ages to come He might show the exceeding riches of His grace in His kindness toward us through Christ Jesus.

Some scholars, like Y. Ezekiel of India, believe that after many years the occupants of the New Jerusalem will go back to heaven-the abode of the Father. God has promised Israel that the descendants of Abraham shall inherit His earth for a "thousand generations". The length of one generation is considered to be around forty years. One hundred generations have passed from Abraham until the present.

Isaiah 66:22-23 states: For as the new heavens and the new earth which I will make shall remain before Me, saith the Lord, so shall your seed and your name remain. And it shall come to pass, that from one new moon to another, and from one sabbath to another, shall all flesh come to worship before Me, saith the Lord.

God intended Abraham's descendants to populate the earth, and when it become too thickly populated, God may transfer surplus population to colonize the other galaxies.

THE LONG WAIT

We can find some earnest and deep appeals and prayers in the Bible for the coming of The Lord. The prophet Isaiah said, "Oh, that you would rend the heavens! That You might come down! That the mountains might shake at your presence."

That same fervent prayer closes the book of the Revelation. In Revelation 22:20, Jesus Christ says,"Surely I am coming quickly." The answering prayer of the aged prophet and seer, the apostle John says, "even so, come Lord Jesus."

Oh, my Lord and Savior, please come quickly! In a world of hatred, deceit, death, famine, chaos, pain, sorrow, and tears, come down, come down. Revelation 1:7 says, "Behold, He (Jesus Christ) is coming with clouds, and every eye will see Him, and they also who pierced Him. And all the tribes of the earth will mourn because of Him. Even so, Amen."

One day God will change the heart, transform military weapons into instruments of peace, and rule the nations with a rod of iron (Isaiah 2:4). Muslim Arabs are the traditional enemies of Israel. But they also will be converted, and will acknowledge the true God, Jesus Christ.

Although the changing of the hearts of nations awaits Christ's return, He does change hearts of individuals today. Jesus promises this to all who accept Him. He says, "Peace I leave with you; My peace I give you." (John 14:27). God can forgive your sin. In Christ's death, He made provision for our peace with God. You receive eternal salvation When you accept Jesus Christ as your personal Savior, after confessing your sins. A man's heart must be changed before a man's life can be changed.

Your problems are nothing when you have Christ. The nations of the world will not have peace until Jesus returns. But in Christ, you can have real peace and security even today.
